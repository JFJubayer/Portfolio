About Me

I am a highly curious, self-taught, and self-driven engineering student pursuing my B.Sc. in Engineering at Mymensingh Engineering College (currently in 2nd Year, 2nd Semester). My fascination lies at the intersection of embedded systems, digital logic design on FPGA, aerospace avionics, and low-level software engineering.
